# AI-First Index

AI-First Index measures how ready a company is to work with AI, across six dimensions. Claude interviews you about how your company works today, scores each dimension against a fixed rubric, and writes a short maturity report with your two weakest areas and three priority actions.

## Use it

Ask Claude something like "How AI-ready is our company?" or "Run the AI-First Index for us." Claude asks for a short company profile, then goes through the six dimensions one at a time. Each question has five defined levels, so you pick the one that matches what happens today. Scores are computed by a small script bundled with the plugin, not estimated by the model, so the same answers always give the same result.

## What you get

- A total score out of 100 and a maturity band
- A score for each of the six dimensions
- Concrete next steps for your two weakest dimensions
- Three priority actions

## Data

The plugin does not send data anywhere. It has no connector and makes no network calls. Your answers stay in your Claude conversation, and the scoring script runs on the answers file Claude writes during that conversation.
